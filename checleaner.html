Checleaner

Balances a photo of an instax mini print, then straightens and crops it.

Measurements

Whites and blacks are pulled to fixed targets (238.8 / 2.2), the same ones used on the desktop batches — so anything cleaned here matches that library. Single prints get cropped to true instax proportions; if the photo holds more than one print it is balanced and left whole. The saved file keeps the original photo's capture date and other EXIF data. Everything runs on the phone; no photo leaves it.
